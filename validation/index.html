How the checks work

Every result the calculators produce is compared with an independent reference: the CoolProp property library (reference equations of state), the fluids and ht engineering libraries, published standard examples, and hand calculations. The same checks run inside the calculator every time it opens (Validation tab), so this page is simply that table, extracted from the current build (version 2026.11.12.2). A check passes when the deviation is inside its tolerance; the tolerance reflects the accuracy of the method, not the convenience of the result.

Control Valve Sizing Manager (members edition)

The members tool runs its own self-test of 171 checks every time it opens: 111 against the IEC 60534-2-1 worked examples and the fluids library for liquid, gas, choked, laminar and reducer cases, and for IEC 60534-8-3 / 8-4 noise; 51 for the extensions: the omega two-phase method against numerical homogeneous-equilibrium integration with CoolProp water (within 3 %), installed characteristic, trim staging, ASME B16.34 ratings and FCI 70-2 leakage; and 9 against a commercial sizing program (below). Its Excel workbook reproduces the tool's Kv with live formulas to better than 0.00001 %.

Benchmark against a commercial sizing program

Real plant printouts from CONVAL 7.0 (F.I.R.S.T., 2009) for two high-pressure letdown valves in a urea plant, supplied by a plant engineer: a choked gas at 190 bar a to 20.6 bar a (two valves) and a flashing liquid (vapour pressure 68.6 bar a). At each printed travel the tool's flow for CONVAL's Cv is compared with CONVAL's flow. At CONVAL's reference load (75–100 % travel, where its printout quotes FL and xT) the two agree within 0.17 %, and both flag every point as choked or flashing. At low travel CONVAL raises FL and xT from its valve database (up to FL ≈ 0.96 at 5 % travel), so it gives up to 6 % more flow there; the tool reproduces this when the vendor's FL / xT-versus-travel table is entered. CONVAL's noise figures use the 2000 edition of IEC 60534-8-3 with a vendor low-noise trim and are not comparable with the 2010 edition used by the tool.
